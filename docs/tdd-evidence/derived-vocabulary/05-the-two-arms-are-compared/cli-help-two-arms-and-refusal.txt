sweeps, and four of the five refusals below fire on real pairs of them.

The two directories under .../Temp/axial-809-constructed are CONSTRUCTED for
this evidence, because no real pair of arms differs only in draw count and no
real #809 sweep produced a brief with no usable draw or ran without gate
scoring. They were built by calling the sweep's own `write_sweep_summary`
over `BriefSweepResult`/`DrawOutcome`/`GateReport` values, never by
hand-writing a summary.json:

  draws3-name / draws2-map  one brief S-01, one commit, three draws against
                            two -- the only thing that differs.
  states-name / states-map  two briefs, three draws, one commit. In `map`,
                            S-01's three draws all FAILed, so no record, no
                            gate report and no source count: `missing`. S-02
                            in both arms carries records and no gate reports,
                            which is what `axial brief smoke` writes with
                            `score_gates=False`: `not-scored`.
