=== 1. the flag ===
                       [--grouping {bag,category}]

  --grouping {bag,category}
                        what step 2 groups passages by (issue #829). 'bag' is
                        wording similarity, the current build, written to
                        data/map/<pin>/. 'category' groups by the claim x
                        mechanism cells of the built vocabulary instead,
                        writing a whole variant artifact set to
                        data/map/<pin>-category/ and leaving the default build
                        untouched beside it; it skips the relations stage and
                        refuses to resume across a vocabulary scheme change
                        (default: bag)
