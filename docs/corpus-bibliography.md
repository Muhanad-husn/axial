# Axial corpus bibliography

The 35 sources in the Axial corpus, alphabetical by author or editor surname.
Each entry carries its internal `source_id` so the list can be cross-referenced
with the pipeline.

Bibliographic data was recovered from the source files themselves — embedded PDF
metadata, title pages, and copyright pages. Fields that could not be confirmed
from a file are marked `[not recovered]` rather than guessed. Where a field was
filled from outside the file, the entry says so explicitly.

Not every item is a standalone scholarly monograph. Edited collections, a
journal article, and a master's research paper are flagged in the notes.

## Notes on the corpus

**Composition.** 33 of the 35 items are books, all held complete. Of the 33,
seven are edited collections rather than single-author monographs (Beshara; Hall
and Schroeder; Heydemann ×2; Kao and Lust; Malešević and Haugaard), leaving 26
single-author monographs. Four of the 33 are volumes of a single work: Mann,
*Sources of Social Power* volumes 1–4.

The remaining two items are not books at all:

- Malešević, "Do Civil Wars Make or Break States?" — a journal article in
  *Theory and Society*.
- Kandiah — an unpublished master's research paper.

**Unreliable embedded metadata.** PDF metadata was cross-checked against title
pages, and in one case contradicted it outright: the Heydemann *War,
Institutions, and Social Change* file carries embedded metadata attributing it to
Michael Hanby, *Augustine and Modernity* — a different book entirely, left over
from the tool that produced the PDF. Two further files carry the placeholder
author "NullObject" or a scanning-tool title. Embedded metadata alone is not a
safe basis for citation here.
